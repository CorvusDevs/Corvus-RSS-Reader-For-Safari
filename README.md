A native **Safari Web Extension** for **iPhone, iPad, and Mac**. Subscribe to your favorite websites, YouTube channels, and blogs, and read everything in one clean, distraction-free interface. Corvus RSS Reader is not a Chrome extension ported to Safari. It's built specifically for Safari with native performance, no background processes, and zero data collection.

## Features

- **RSS & Atom Feeds.** Subscribe to any website that publishes a feed. Corvus auto-discovers feeds on the page you're visiting, with fast continuous refresh and built-in feed health tools.
- **YouTube Channels.** Subscribe to any YouTube channel directly from its page. Handles and custom URLs are resolved automatically, and you can hide Shorts to focus on full videos.
- **Folder Organization.** Group feeds into folders and nested sub-folders. Keep Tech, News, YouTube, and Design neatly separated.
- **5 View Modes.** Magazine, list, card, expanded, and full-view layouts. Choose the reading experience that works best for you.
- **Star, Pin & Read Later.** Save articles for later, star your favorites, and pin important items. Built-in smart feeds for quick access.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts.** Navigate, star, refresh, and manage articles entirely from the keyboard. J/K to browse, S to star, V to open, and more.
- **Full-Text Search.** Search across all your articles by title, content, or author. Find anything instantly, even in thousands of articles.
- **Auto-Categorize.** New feeds are automatically sorted into 20 category folders based on domain and keyword matching. No manual organization needed.
- **OPML Import & Export.** Bring your feeds from any reader with OPML import. Full folder hierarchy preserved, with progress tracking and duplicate detection.
- **Offline Reading.** Save articles with embedded images for reading without an internet connection. Your saved content is always available.
- **Feed Health Dashboard.** Monitor broken, stale, and error-prone feeds at a glance. Bulk disable inactive feeds and retry disabled ones with a single tap.
- **Auto-Action Rules.** Create rules to automatically star, tag, mute, or archive articles based on keywords, feeds, or authors. Your inbox, your rules.
- **Smart Filters.** Built-in smart feeds for Today, Unread, Starred, Read Later, and Archived articles. Jump to what matters in one click.
- **Discover Feeds.** Browse a curated directory of 4,800+ feeds across 34 categories and 47 countries. Find new sources in seconds, no URL hunting required.
- **Themes & Customization.** Pick from a visual grid of themes (Light, Dark, Midnight, Sepia, Nord, Dracula, Solarized Dark), plus custom accent colors, font sizes, density settings, and newspaper-style column layouts.
- **Organized Settings.** A polished settings panel with 8 categories, live search across every option, and a What's New sheet that surfaces release notes on first launch after every update.
- **33 languages.** Fully localized including the settings UI and onboarding, with right-to-left layout for Arabic and Hebrew.

## What makes Corvus RSS Reader special

**Built for Safari, not ported to it.** No Chromium shim, no background daemon. It uses Safari's native extension APIs, so it stays fast and light on Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

**Subscribe to YouTube like any feed.** Open a channel, hit subscribe, and its uploads land in your reader. Handles and custom URLs resolve automatically, and you can hide Shorts to keep only full videos.

**A directory, not a blank search box.** Discover Feeds ships a curated catalog of 4,800+ sources across 34 categories and 47 countries, so finding something to read takes seconds instead of URL hunting.

**Reading the way you like it.** Five view modes, seven themes, custom accent colors, adjustable density, and newspaper-style columns. The reader bends to your taste, not the other way around.

**Rules that tidy up for you.** Auto-categorization files new feeds into the right folder, and auto-action rules star, tag, mute, or archive articles automatically based on your own conditions.

**33 languages, fully translated.** Including the in-app settings, the onboarding, and this site. RTL layout for Arabic and Hebrew. The language picker is one tap away.

**Built for privacy.** No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no network requests beyond fetching your feeds. Everything runs locally in Safari. Your reading stays completely private.

## Built with

**Safari Web Extension MV3** · **WebKit** · **SwiftUI**

## Privacy

No account required. No sign-ups, no cloud sync, no tracking. Your feeds and articles live entirely on your device in `browser.storage.local`. Corvus RSS Reader collects zero data and never phones home.
