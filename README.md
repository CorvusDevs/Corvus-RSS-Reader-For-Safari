## Features

- **Subscribe without hunting for URLs.** Corvus finds RSS and Atom feeds on the page you are visiting, including YouTube channel feeds.
- **Discover something new.** Browse more than 5,000 curated feeds across 34 categories and 47 countries.
- **Read your way.** Switch between magazine, list, card, expanded, and full-view layouts, with seven themes and custom accents.
- **Keep a large library tidy.** Use folders, nested folders, smart filters, tags, full-text search, and automatic categorization.
- **Let rules handle repetitive work.** Automatically star, tag, mute, or archive matching articles.
- **Take articles offline.** Save complete articles with their images for later reading.
- **Know when a source needs attention.** Feed Health identifies broken, stale, and error-prone subscriptions.
- **Bring your existing library.** Import and export OPML while preserving folders and detecting duplicates.

## Install

1. Download Corvus from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761442829).
2. Open Safari Settings, select Extensions, and enable Corvus.
3. Visit a website or YouTube channel, open Corvus, and subscribe.

The same purchase supports Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

## What makes Corvus different

**Made for Safari.** Corvus uses Safari extension APIs and a native Apple host app instead of carrying a Chromium runtime or background daemon.

**A directory, not a blank search box.** Discover offers curated sources by topic and country, so a new reader can build a useful library in minutes.

**Local by design.** Feeds, articles, settings, and reading state remain on your device. Corvus only connects to the sources you choose to follow.

**Useful on every Apple screen.** The desktop reader supports dense multi-column workflows, while the mobile interface uses touch-safe navigation and compact discovery.

## Requirements

## Privacy

No account, sign-up, telemetry, advertising, or analytics. Corvus stores your feeds and articles locally in Safari and makes no network requests beyond retrieving the sources you subscribe to. Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://corvusdevs.github.io/Corvus-RSS-Reader-For-Safari/privacy.html).
